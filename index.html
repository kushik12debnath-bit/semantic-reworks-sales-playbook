Phase 01 · Opener
Pattern-Interrupt Introduction
PRIMARY OPENER
YOU SAY →
"Hi, is this [Name]? Good — I'll be upfront, this is a cold call, so I'll keep it to two minutes max. My name is [Your Name] from Semantic Reworks. We build websites for businesses in [City/Industry] — specifically ones that actually bring in customers rather than just sit there looking pretty. I just want to ask you one quick question. Is that okay?"
IF THEY SAY "WHAT'S THE QUESTION?"
BRIDGE →
"When someone searches for [what they sell] in [their city] on Google right now — does your business show up, or does a competitor take that customer?"
💡
Why this works: The Google question makes the pain instantly real and personal. They either know they don't rank (pain confirmed) or they're not sure (curiosity opened). Either way, you're in the conversation.
🔍
Phase 02 · Discovery
3 Questions That Reveal the Real Pain
Q1 — CURRENT SITUATION
"Right now — do you have a website, or is your business mainly running through referrals and social media?"
Q2 — THE RESULT QUESTION
"If you do have a site — roughly how many enquiries or leads does it bring you in a month? Even a ballpark is fine."
Q3 — THE VISION QUESTION
"If your website was converting 5–10 new enquiries every month on autopilot — how would that change things for your business?"
🎙️
Listen for: "We get nothing from it", "it's outdated", "my nephew built it", "we rely on word of mouth". These phrases are your green light to pivot to the value pitch.
⚡
Phase 03 · The Fork
Two Paths — Exact Wording
IF THEY RESIST — "We already have a website / don't need one"
"Totally fair — most businesses do have something in place. My question is: is that site actively bringing you new enquiries, or is it more of a digital business card that people glance at and leave? Because those are very different things. We don't rebuild for the sake of it — we only take on a project if we can show you a clear reason why the current one is costing you customers. Would you be open to a 10-minute free audit — I'll tell you exactly what's working and what isn't, no obligation."
IF THEY ADMIT THE PROBLEM — "Yeah it doesn't do much"
"That's actually very common — and it's almost never the business's fault. Most websites are built by someone who knows how to make things look good but has no idea about how Google works or how to turn a visitor into an enquiry. That's the exact gap we close. We build sites that rank, load fast, and have one job — to get someone to contact you. Can I show you what that looks like for a business similar to yours? Takes 20 minutes, completely free."
💎
Phase 04 · Value Pitch
2-Sentence Bridge — No Jargon
THE PITCH →
"At Semantic Reworks, we build high-performance websites for growing businesses — designed to rank on Google, load in under 2 seconds, and turn visitors into paying customers. Unlike most web agencies, we focus on what happens after launch — the SEO, the conversion flow, the calls-to-action — so your site works as a full-time salesperson, not a brochure."
⚠️
Never say: React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, responsive design, UI/UX on the first call. Clients buy outcomes — more customers, more calls, more revenue. Technology is for the proposal stage.
🔒
Phase 05 · The Close
Lock In the Next Step
PRIMARY CLOSE
SAY THIS →
"Here's what I'd suggest — let me do a free 15-minute website audit of your current online presence and show you exactly what's holding it back. I'll screen-share the whole thing so you can see it live. If you like what you hear, we can talk about next steps. If not, you'll at least know what to fix. What does your schedule look like — does Tuesday or Wednesday work better for you?"
IF THEY WANT TO THINK ABOUT IT
SOFT BRIDGE →
"No problem at all. Let me send you a quick before/after of a website we rebuilt for a [similar business type] — shows the difference in enquiries before and after. Takes 30 seconds to look at. What's the best number to send it on — WhatsApp?"

Objection Vault
Every Website Objection + Exact Response
OBJECTIONEXACT RESPONSE
"We already have a website." "Great — is it bringing you new enquiries, or is it more of a digital brochure people glance at and leave? Having a site and having a site that works are two very different things. Would you be open to a free 10-minute check to see which category yours falls into?"
"It's too expensive." "Completely fair. Can I ask — how many new customers would your business need from the website each month to make it worthwhile? Because once we work that number out, the investment usually looks very different. Our entry package starts at [price] — want me to break down the ROI?"
"We get customers from referrals, we don't need a website." "That's a great position to be in — referrals are the best kind. My question is: when those referrals go to look you up online before calling, what do they find? If the answer is nothing or something outdated, you might be losing 20–30% of them before they ever contact you."
"My nephew / someone already built one for us." "That's really common — and there's nothing wrong with that as a starting point. The issue is usually that it was built to look good but not to rank on Google or convert visitors. Does your current site show up when someone searches [their service] in [city]?"
"I can build it on Wix/Shopify myself." "You absolutely can — those platforms are great for getting something up quickly. The gap usually shows up in the SEO and conversion side: Google tends to rank custom-built sites significantly higher, and the lead-capture flow needs specific design decisions that templates don't make for you."
"We don't have budget right now." "Understood. When does budget typically open up for you — end of quarter, new financial year? I'll note that down and reach out then. In the meantime, would it help to have the audit done now so you know exactly what you're looking at when the timing is right?"
"Send me your portfolio / pricing." "Absolutely — sending it over now. To make sure what I send is actually relevant: is your priority getting found on Google, getting more enquiries from the site, or both? That way I can share the most relevant examples."
"We tried a web agency before and it was a bad experience." "I'm sorry to hear that — honestly it's more common than it should be. Can I ask what went wrong? Because if it was delays, lack of communication, or a site that never ranked — those are the specific things I'd want to show you we do differently before asking for a minute more of your time."
"I need to think about it." "Of course. Let me make it easy — I'll send you one short case study of a similar business and a rough idea of what a project would look like. No commitment, no follow-up unless you ask. If after that it still doesn't feel right, I'll close the file — fair?"
"We're too small / just starting out." "That's actually the best time to do it — building the right digital foundation from day one is far cheaper than fixing a bad one later. We have a starter package specifically designed for early-stage businesses. Want to see what that includes?"
